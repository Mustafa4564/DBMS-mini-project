## 🎯 Common Questions Answered

### Q: Do I need to install anything?
**A**: No! Just open index.html in your browser. No installations needed.

### Q: Where is my data stored?
**A**: In your browser's LocalStorage. All data stays on your computer.

### Q: Can I deploy online?
**A**: Yes! Follow GITHUB_PAGES.md (10 minutes to go live for free).

### Q: Can I modify it?
**A**: Yes! The code is yours. Customize colors, add features, etc.

### Q: Is it secure?
**A**: Your data never leaves your computer. Completely private.

### Q: How much data can I store?
**A**: About 5MB, which is enough for thousands of products.

### Q: What if my browser closes?
**A**: Your data is saved! It persists when you reopen the app.

### Q: Can multiple people use it?
**A**: Currently single-user. Future versions will support multi-user.

### Q: How do I backup my data?
**A**: Data persists in browser until cache is cleared. Export feature coming soon.

### Q: Can I use it offline?
**A**: Yes! Once loaded, it works without internet.
